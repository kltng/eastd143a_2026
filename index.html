Digital humanities

關於 About

I work on the unglamorous infrastructure of Chinese historical research: getting sources out of images, into databases, and into a form a researcher — or an agent — can actually query.

That means calendars, because a Ming date is not a Gregorian date and the intercalary months disagree between sources. It means OCR, because 古籍 defeat pipelines built for modern print. And it means catalogues, because a subject heading is only useful if you can find the right one.

Most of it ends up as small, boring, well-documented tools that other people can install. A few of them are on PyPI. I teach some of this too.
